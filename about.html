ABOUT TASKLY

Rebuilding Community Bonds Through Equal Time Exchange.

Taskly is a mutual aid cooperative reimagining personal concierge care. We believe everyone has valuable skills to offer and everyone deserves a helping hand with life's daily errands—without commercial price barriers.

Explore Our Journey
OUR MILESTONES

The Evolution of Taskly (2021 – 2026)

2021 • INCEPTION

Started as Neighborhood Support Circle

Taskly began as a grassroots volunteer group organizing grocery deliveries, pharmacy runs, and porch check-ins for elderly residents.

2023 • TIME BANK LAUNCH

Adopted 1-to-1 Time Banking Standard

Formally transitioned into a cooperative time bank, reaching 25,000 exchanged hours where 1 hour of help equals 1 time credit for all tasks.

2025 • EXPANSION

12 Neighborhood Chapters & Senior Concierge

Expanded across urban districts with dedicated chapters for doctor appointment rides, pet sitting, tech literacy, and home decluttering.

2026 • DIGITAL PLATFORM

Cooperative Member Dashboard & Secure Messaging

Launched our real-time member portal, empowering over 4,800 neighbors to track time credits, coordinate tasks, and manage skills seamlessly.

OUR CORE PHILOSOPHY

4 Core Values Guiding Every Errand Exchange

We are rooted in the time banking philosophy of mutual respect, equality, and radical community collaboration.

Browse Available Skills
Equality of Time

Every member's time is valued identically: 60 minutes equals 1 credit, whether giving a ride or organizing paperwork.

Mutual Trust & Safety

All members pass identity checks and abide by our kindness charter to ensure respectful, worry-free exchanges.

Intergenerational Bond

We bridge generations by pairing young professionals with seniors for companionship, tech setup, and grocery runs.

100% Cash-Free Economy

No money transfers take place. Services are exchanged purely on reciprocity, making personal concierge help accessible to all.
