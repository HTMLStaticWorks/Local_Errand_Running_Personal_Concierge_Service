How Clara Got Weekly Grocery Runs in Exchange for Conversational French Lessons

When 78-year-old Clara could no longer drive to the central market, she listed 1-hour French conversation practice on Taskly. Her neighbor Lucas, studying for his master's degree, picks up her produce every Tuesday in exchange for her tutoring hours.

"Time banking restored my independence. I am not asking for charity; I am sharing my lifetime of knowledge in return for help I genuinely need."
