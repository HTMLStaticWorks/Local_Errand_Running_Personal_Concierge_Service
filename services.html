COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE BASE

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Get quick answers to the most common questions regarding our errand services, booking processes, safety measures, and pricing.

Booking is simple! Log in to your member dashboard, select the errand category you need (such as Grocery Shopping, Pickup & Drop-off, or Pharmacy collection), specify your pickup and delivery locations with your timing preferences, and submit the request. Nearby verified runners receive your task alert and accept it within minutes. You can communicate directly through our secure in-app chat throughout the errand.

You can provide your shopping list with preferred brands, quantities, and dietary specifications through our dashboard. For purchases, members either prepay stores for curbside collection or provide funds through our secure escrow checkout. When in-store, your runner can message photos for item substitution approvals and will always provide a photo of the itemized receipt upon delivery.

Yes! Multi-stop errands are fully supported under our Express & Multi-Stop Dispatch tier. For example, a runner can pick up your dry cleaning, stop by the post office, and grab a bakery order in a single coordinated route. Simply list your stops in the preferred order when posting the errand.

For prepaid courier returns (UPS, FedEx, USPS, Amazon), you can attach the digital QR code or tape the printed label to your packaged parcel. For in-store merchandise returns or size exchanges, your runner will take your original store receipt and exchange instructions, process the counter transaction, and immediately upload a photo of the return confirmation slip to your dashboard.

Absolutely. Prescription pickups follow all state and pharmacy compliance rules. You notify your pharmacy that an authorized Taskly representative is picking up your prepared medication. All medications remain sealed in tamper-evident pharmacy packaging, handled with strict confidentiality, and delivered directly to you with prompt delivery confirmation.

Document deliveries are handled exclusively by top-rated, ID-verified runners. The courier travels directly from the pickup point to the delivery address without unmonitored stops. Recipients must provide a digital signature or one-time confirmation code to complete the hand-off, ensuring sensitive papers never go missing.

Taskly is founded on an equal time bank: 1 hour of volunteer help earned equals 1 time credit to spend on any errand service. For non-credit commercial requests or members wishing to pay directly, commercial rates are listed with clearly marked placeholders (such as [Placeholder: $25.00/hr]), allowing total flexibility whether using reciprocal hours or standard booking.
