HOW IT WORKS

Community Care Powered by Time.

Time banking is simple and equal: 1 hour spent helping a neighbor earns you 1 time credit. Use your earned credits to receive help whenever you need an errand done.

Join the Time Bank
1 Hour = 1 Time Credit Equal Principle
All skills valued equally • No money needed
STAGE 01 POST REQUEST

1. Request or Offer Help

Post what you need—like prescription pickup, dog walking, or closet decluttering—or offer your availability.

STAGE 02 • ACTIVE COMMUNITY MATCH

2. Reliable Neighbor Care

A verified neighbor accepts the errand, communicates securely via dashboard chat, and completes the task.

STAGE 03 TIME DEPOSITED

3. Credits Deposited

Time credits are instantly added to the helper's balance to spend anytime on future errands or skills.

SAFETY & TRUST GUARANTEE

Built on Cooperative Safety, Trust & Equality

COMMUNITY IDENTITY

Vetted & Verified Local Neighbor Profiles

Every member goes through ID verification, local address confirmation, and community orientation to ensure safe, dependable, and warm errand exchanges.

ID Verified & Code of Conduct 100% Safe Exchanges

1 Hour = 1 Credit Parity

No matter the skill, every person's hour is equally valuable. An hour of yard raking equals an hour of bookkeeping.

Zero Money Exchanged

Eliminate stressful market pricing. Only direct, reciprocal time banking credits are used between members.

Two-Way Community Rating & Feedback

Both the requester and service provider leave mutual reviews after each exchange, maintaining highest standards of kindness and punctuality.
